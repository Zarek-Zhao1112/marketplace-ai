# F1: Newegg入驻模式

## Findings

### [1] Newegg是纯Marketplace模式，不存在独立的"Official Store"入驻通道
- quote: "Newegg Marketplace – More Customers. Higher Sales. One Location. Low Commission Fees."
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high

### [2] 所有卖家统一为Marketplace Seller，按品类收取8-15%佣金
- quote: "Commission Rates: Computer Hardware 10%, Consumer Electronics 9%, Software 15%, Unlocked Cell Phones 8%, Video Game Consoles 8%"
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-23
- confidence: high

### [3] 新卖家前90天享受6%优惠佣金率（低于标准费率）
- quote: "During the first 90 days, eligible sellers will receive a rebate equal to the difference between their standard commission rate and 6%, resulting in an effective 6% commission rate"
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-01
- confidence: high

### [4] 会员等级决定功能权限，非入驻模式差异
- quote: "Enterprise: $99.95/month | Professional: $49.95/month | Non-Elite: $0.99/Unique Item Sold"
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/index.php/elite-seller/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high

### [5] 品牌方可通过"Claim Brand Ownership"获得Verified认证标识
- quote: "A Brand Ownership Request is for brand manufacturers who join the platform, but they are not the first organization to sell their products on Newegg Marketplace."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [6] Brand Ownership提供"Verified"徽章和"Direct from Manufacturer"筛选
- quote: "'Verified' badging on the product page, so customers have peace of mind when shopping that they are purchasing from a direct source... The ability for customers to filter search results by 'Direct from Manufacturer'."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [7] 品牌所有权认证需要上传商标/版权/专利证明，审核周期7天
- quote: "Upload the following information: Manufacturer Logo, Manufacturer Introduction, Subcategory List, Proof of Ownership (supporting documentation may include Trademarks, Copyrights, or patents)... it can take up to 7 days before the approval/denial letter"
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [8] Premium Store功能仅对Professional和Enterprise会员开放
- quote: "Available for Professional and Enterprise members. Please review our Newegg Elite Seller program to learn more about it."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/manage-premium-store/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-08-07
- confidence: high

### [9] 入驻门槛：需提交营业执照、所有权信息、财务信息，经审核+承保审查
- quote: "After submitting your seller registration, Newegg will assign an account manager to review and approve your application... Following approval, our underwriting team will review your account information."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/seller-account-registration/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-08-12
- confidence: high

### [10] 中国卖家入驻需联系品类经理，佣金费率单独协商
- quote: "For sellers looking to sell into China, please reach out to your category manager or fill out our contact form as rates will differ."
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high
