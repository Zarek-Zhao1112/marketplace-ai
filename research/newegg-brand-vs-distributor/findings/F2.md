# F2: 品牌方vs经销商优劣势

## Findings

### [1] 品牌方通过Claim Brand Ownership获得"Verified"徽章和"Direct from Manufacturer"筛选入口，经销商无法获取
- quote: "'Verified' badging on the product page, so customers have peace of mind when shopping that they are purchasing from a direct source... The ability for customers can filter search results by 'Direct from Manufacturer'."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [2] 品牌方拥有独家"回复产品评论"权限，经销商只能查看不能回复
- quote: "Only eligible brand sellers have the ability to reply to product reviews. Other users can only view this list."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [3] GPU品类实施严格品牌授权控制，仅允许约20个品牌（NVIDIA/AMD显卡），未授权品牌禁止在Marketplace销售
- quote: "Any other GPUs not listed below are not allowed on the Newegg Marketplace."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/brand-authorization/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-04-15
- confidence: high

### [4] 品牌方可通过Brand Authorization系统控制哪些经销商可以销售其产品，形成准入壁垒
- quote: "Newegg enables you to authorize and sell specific brands effortlessly... Categories necessary for that brand will be made available when the brand is approved for your account."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/brand-authorization/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-04-15
- confidence: high

### [5] 品牌方独家功能：Syndicated Reviews（将品牌官网评论同步到Newegg），经销商无法使用
- quote: "Brands and manufacturers that are eligible can syndicate their reviews on Newegg. This is only for sellers who are the brand owner."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/brand-authorization/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-04-15
- confidence: high

### [6] Newegg卖家分三个等级：Non-Elite(100个SKU上限)、Professional(6000 SKU)、Enterprise(12000 SKU)，经销商从Non-Elite起步限制巨大
- quote: "Enterprise: $99.95/month | Professional: $49.95/month | Non-Elite: $0.99/Unique Item Sold"
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/index.php/elite-seller/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high

### [7] Lenovo作为品牌方直接入驻Newegg，目标是成为#1 PC公司，Newegg销售是关键渠道
- quote: "Being the #1 PC company in the US is our ultimate objective and sales growth via the Newegg Marketplace is critical to achieving this."
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high

### [8] 品牌方入驻后可获得Newegg Media Services（直播、A+内容、社媒视频）等定制化营销支持
- quote: "Showcase best sellers on Newegg livestreams or work with our in-house studio team to create A+ content, product images, and vertical videos for social media."
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high

### [9] 非品牌卖家面临价格战风险——同一产品多个经销商竞争BuyBox，而品牌方通过Verified徽章获得消费者信任溢价
- quote: "Verified badging on the search results pages, which stands out from the crowd to let browsing customers know they can trust your product offering above the rest."
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [10] 新卖家前90天享受6%优惠佣金率，但品牌方可能因已有渠道利润更高而不重视Newegg新渠道
- quote: "During the first 90 days, eligible sellers will receive a rebate equal to the difference between their standard commission rate and 6%"
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04
- confidence: medium

### [11] 品牌方Claim Brand Ownership需提交商标/版权/专利证明，审核周期7天，小BD难以帮助品牌方快速完成认证
- quote: "Upload the following information: Manufacturer Logo, Manufacturer Introduction, Subcategory List, Proof of Ownership... it can take up to 7 days before the approval/denial letter"
- url: https://sellerportal.newegg.com/selleracademy/knowledge-base/claim-brand-ownership-request/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-09
- confidence: high

### [12] Montech作为相对小众品牌通过Newegg Sponsored Product Ads在9个月内销量增长超50%，验证了Newegg对中小品牌的增长价值
- quote: "Within 9 months of using Sponsored Product Ads, we exceeded our sales expectations, growing sales by at least 50%."
- url: https://www.newegg.com/sellers/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high
